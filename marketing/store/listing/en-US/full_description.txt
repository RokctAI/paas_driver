Driver puts your delivery day in one app. Go online and drive: your zone, your current delivery and your next job all live on one map. Take the next job: orders ready nearby appear for you to accept with one tap. Your route lines up the stops, pickups first and then drop-offs, with the distance and the cash to collect at each one. Parcels ride along too: documents and packages, with the sender, receiver and fee on every parcel. Pick one up nearby to add it to your run, and keep each parcel's history. Keep your cash straight: see your wallet balance and every deposit, whether approved, rejected or waiting. Sell at the door when a customer wants more: pick what they take, collect at the load price, and the van updates itself. Return what is unsold before you close the load. Draw your delivery zone on the map, and dispatch follows the outline you set. Check your earnings today, this week or this month, and keep your account details up to date. Driver is the delivery app of a white-label delivery platform, for the drivers who work its routes.
